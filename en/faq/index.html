Everything about the Christmas camp

Updated: 18 November 2026 · Source: Time Chamber

What is Time Chamber's Christmas Work camp?

Christmas Work is Time Chamber's Christmas camp in Barcelona: seven mornings of individual basketball skills training (ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset) with Coach Iandrey Panisa's method, on 22-24 and 28-31 December 2026, from 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome.

When is the Time Chamber Christmas camp in 2026?

There are two weeks: week 1 is 22, 23 and 24 December (Tuesday to Thursday) and week 2 is 28 to 31 December (Monday to Thursday). Hours are 9:00 to 13:00 every day. You can join one week or both.

Where is the camp? Is it at the Horta Velodrome?

Yes. The Christmas camp takes place at the Horta Velodrome (Velòdrom d'Horta), Passeig de la Vall d'Hebron, in the Horta-Guinardó district of Barcelona. It is one of Time Chamber's regular training venues in the city.

How much does the Christmas Work camp cost?

Week 1 (3 days) costs €96, week 2 (4 days) costs €128 and both weeks together (7 days) cost €179. You book with the form on this page and the Time Chamber team then contacts you to confirm payment.

What Christmas basketball skills training is there in Barcelona?

At Christmas, Time Chamber runs the Christmas Work camp: basketball skills training in Barcelona on 22-24 and 28-31 December, from 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome. Players work on ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset, in groups by age and level.

Is Christmas Work a Christmas basketball showcase?

It is not a showcase tournament with scouts: Christmas Work is a skills camp. If you are looking for a Christmas showcase in Barcelona, it is the way to arrive prepared: seven mornings of individual work with the Time Chamber method to improve ball handling, shooting and 1-on-1.

What is Iandrey Panisa's camp like?

Iandrey Panisa's camp is the Time Chamber method applied in a group: individual technical work, lots of quality reps and high-level standards, adapted to each age. Iandrey has trained players such as Serge Ibaka, Jarred Vanderbilt and Willy Hernangómez.

What ages is the Christmas camp for?

It is designed for youth players who already play basketball or want to start skills training. The form asks for age and level so each participant is grouped with players of a similar age and level.

What should players bring?

Basketball kit and trainers, a water bottle and the will to work. If the player has allergies or needs medication, please say so in the registration form.

How do I sign up for the Christmas Work camp?

Fill in the registration form on this page with the player's details and the responsible adult's details, and choose week 1, week 2 or both. Time Chamber will contact you to confirm the place and payment. You can also message us on WhatsApp at +34 697 635 183.

Is there a basketball camp in Barcelona at Christmas 2026?

Yes. Time Chamber runs Christmas Work, a Christmas basketball camp in Barcelona: seven mornings of skills training on 22–24 and 28–31 December 2026, from 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome. You can book one week (€96 or €128) or both (€179).

What is basketball skills training (tecnificación) and how is it different from a camp?

Skills training, called tecnificación in Spain, is practice focused on a player's individual technique: ball handling, shooting, finishing and 1-on-1, with many repetitions and correction. A regular camp mixes games and leisure. Christmas Work is a skills camp: every morning is spent improving technique.

How much does the Time Chamber Christmas camp cost per day?

It works out at €32 per morning for a single week: €96 for week 1 (3 days) and €128 for week 2 (4 days). Both weeks together cost €179 for 7 days, about €25.60 per morning, which is €45 less than booking them separately.

What do players train each morning at the Christmas Work camp?

Each morning, from 9:00 to 13:00, players work on the four pillars of the Time Chamber method: ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset. It is individual technical work, with many quality repetitions and a level of demand adapted to the age of each group.

Can boys and girls from any club join?

Yes. Christmas Work is open to boys and girls from any club, and to players who are not in a club. The sign-up form asks for club, age and level so that each participant is grouped with players of the same age and level.

My child is just starting to play: is the camp right for them?

Yes. Groups are made by age and level, so beginners work on the fundamentals (dribbling, shooting and passing) with players at their level, while those who already compete train at a higher intensity. You state the level on the sign-up form.

Who is Iandrey Panisa?

Iandrey Panisa, known as Coach Iandrey, is the founder and head coach of Time Chamber, a basketball skills academy in Barcelona. He has worked on the individual technique of professionals such as Serge Ibaka, Jarred Vanderbilt and Willy Hernangómez, and he leads the Christmas Work camp.

Which professional players have trained with Time Chamber?

Players who have trained with Coach Iandrey include Serge Ibaka, Jarred Vanderbilt, Willy Hernangómez, Donatas Motiejūnas, Thomas Heurtel, Mariona Ortiz and Joel Parra. Videos of those sessions are published on Time Chamber's Instagram and on the academy page of this website.

Who organises the Christmas Work camp?

It is organised solely by Time Chamber, the basketball skills academy founded by Iandrey Panisa in Barcelona. The programme, the coaches and the method are Time Chamber's own, and you can only sign up on this website or by WhatsApp at +34 697 635 183.

How do I get to the Horta Velodrome by metro?

The Horta Velodrome (Velòdrom d'Horta) is on Passeig de la Vall d'Hebron, in the Horta-Guinardó district of Barcelona, next to Mundet metro station on line 3 (L3, green). It is a few minutes' walk from the metro exit. The camp starts at 9:00.

Can a player from outside Barcelona or from another country join?

Yes. The camp takes place in Barcelona, in the mornings only (9:00 to 13:00), and players from outside the city and from other countries can sign up. This website is available in six languages; for questions about your trip, message us on WhatsApp at +34 697 635 183.

Who signs up a minor, and what happens to their data and photos?

A parent or legal guardian signs up the player. Data is used only to manage the camp, health data requires explicit consent, and photos and videos of the minor are used only if the family authorises it. The full details are on the legal page.

How do I contact Time Chamber to ask about the camp?

By WhatsApp at +34 697 635 183, with the chat button on every page of this website, or by email at time.chamber.es@gmail.com. You can also leave your details on the sign-up form and Time Chamber will contact you.
