2. Privacy policy (GDPR and LOPDGDD)

We process data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Act 3/2018 on personal data protection and digital rights (LOPDGDD).

Data controller

TIMECHAMBER S.L., NIF B13887153, Pg. de la Vall d'Hebron, 185, 08035 Barcelona. Privacy contact: time.chamber.es@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • About the player: first name, surname, age, club, level, T-shirt size.
  • About the responsible adult: full name, email and phone.
  • Receipt of the payment transfer.
  • People authorised to collect the child, if provided.
  • Health data (allergies, medication), only if the family provides it.
  • Images from the camp, only if authorised.

Children's data

Participants are minors. Registration and all consents are given by the holder of parental responsibility or guardianship (art. 8 GDPR and art. 7 LOPDGDD), who declares that they are entitled to do so. We do not collect data directly from minors.

Who has access to the data

We do not sell or share data with third parties, except where legally required or in a medical emergency. Our technology providers have access as processors, under contract: Google (forms, spreadsheets, email and analytics), Salesforce (registration management) and web hosting. Some may process data outside the European Economic Area; in that case the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses apply.

How long we keep it

  • Registration data: during the camp and afterwards for the legal periods for tax and liability obligations.
  • Health data: deleted within 30 days of the end of the camp.
  • Unfinished registrations: a maximum of 30 days.
  • Images: until consent is withdrawn.

Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw any consent at any time, by writing to time.chamber.es@gmail.com and stating the participant's name. If you believe your request has not been dealt with, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es). We do not make automated decisions or carry out profiling.

3. Child protection (LOPIVI)

Time Chamber applies Spanish Organic Act 8/2021 on the comprehensive protection of children and adolescents against violence (LOPIVI) and Organic Act 1/1996 on the legal protection of minors.

  • Staff: all staff in contact with children have provided a clear certificate from the Spanish Central Register of Sex Offenders (art. 57 LOPIVI).
  • Child protection officer: Iandrey Panisa · time.chamber.es@gmail.com · +34 666 095 447. This is the person to contact with any concern about how a child is being treated.
  • Reporting channel: any family or player can report a risk situation to time.chamber.es@gmail.com or +34 666 095 447. Reports are handled confidentially.
  • Protocol: at any sign of violence, bullying or abuse we act immediately, protect the child, inform the family and, where appropriate, the authorities (arts. 15 and 16 LOPIVI).
  • Drop-off and pick-up: children are only handed over to the responsible adult or to the people authorised in the form. They only leave alone if the family expressly authorises it.
  • Good treatment: physical punishment and humiliation are not allowed. Staff do not communicate privately with minors through social media or messaging; contact is always with the responsible adult.
  • Images: images of minors are only published with the family's written consent, without full names, and are removed on request.

Helplines in Spain: 116 111 (children and adolescents) · 112 (emergencies).

4. Camp terms
  • Activity: Christmas Work basketball skills camp, 22-24 and 28-31 December 2026, 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome (Barcelona).
  • Price: week 1, €96; week 2, €128; both weeks, €179. Taxes included.
  • Registration and place: sending the form is a request. The place is confirmed when Time Chamber confirms it and payment is received. Places are limited and allocated in order of payment.
  • Payment: bank transfer at the time of registration, to the account shown on the form. The family attaches the receipt when registering.
  • Cancellation and refunds: if the family cancels, the fee is refunded only when the participant cannot attend because of illness or injury, supported by a medical report. For any other reason there is no refund.
  • Withdrawal: as this is a leisure service on specific dates, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply (art. 103.l of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007).
  • Changes: if the camp cannot take place for reasons beyond Time Chamber's control, another date or a refund will be offered.
  • Insurance: Time Chamber holds civil liability and accident insurance covering the activity.
  • Health: the family reports allergies, medication or injuries. In an emergency we contact the family and, if needed, the emergency services (112).
  • Rules: respect for other players, staff and facilities. Serious misconduct may lead to exclusion without refund.
  • Personal belongings: Time Chamber is not responsible for the loss of valuables.
