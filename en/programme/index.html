04 / FAQ

Everything about the Christmas camp

Updated: 18 November 2026 · Source: Time Chamber

What is Time Chamber's Christmas Work camp?

Christmas Work is Time Chamber's Christmas camp in Barcelona: seven mornings of individual basketball skills training (ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset) with Coach Iandrey Panisa's method, on 22-24 and 28-31 December 2026, from 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome.

What is basketball skills training (tecnificación) and how is it different from a camp?

Skills training, called tecnificación in Spain, is practice focused on a player's individual technique: ball handling, shooting, finishing and 1-on-1, with many repetitions and correction. A regular camp mixes games and leisure. Christmas Work is a skills camp: every morning is spent improving technique.

What do players train each morning at the Christmas Work camp?

Each morning, from 9:00 to 13:00, players work on the four pillars of the Time Chamber method: ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset. It is individual technical work, with many quality repetitions and a level of demand adapted to the age of each group.

Can boys and girls from any club join?

Yes. Christmas Work is open to boys and girls from any club, and to players who are not in a club. The sign-up form asks for club, age and level so that each participant is grouped with players of the same age and level.

My child is just starting to play: is the camp right for them?

Yes. Groups are made by age and level, so beginners work on the fundamentals (dribbling, shooting and passing) with players at their level, while those who already compete train at a higher intensity. You state the level on the sign-up form.

What ages is the Christmas camp for?

It is designed for youth players who already play basketball or want to start skills training. The form asks for age and level so each participant is grouped with players of a similar age and level.

What Christmas basketball skills training is there in Barcelona?

At Christmas, Time Chamber runs the Christmas Work camp: basketball skills training in Barcelona on 22-24 and 28-31 December, from 9:00 to 13:00, at the Horta Velodrome. Players work on ball handling, shooting, 1-on-1 and mindset, in groups by age and level.
