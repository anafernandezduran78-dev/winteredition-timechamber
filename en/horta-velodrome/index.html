02 / FAQ

Everything about the Christmas camp

Updated: 18 November 2026 · Source: Time Chamber

Where is the camp? Is it at the Horta Velodrome?

Yes. The Christmas camp takes place at the Horta Velodrome (Velòdrom d'Horta), Passeig de la Vall d'Hebron, in the Horta-Guinardó district of Barcelona. It is one of Time Chamber's regular training venues in the city.

How do I get to the Horta Velodrome by metro?

The Horta Velodrome (Velòdrom d'Horta) is on Passeig de la Vall d'Hebron, in the Horta-Guinardó district of Barcelona, next to Mundet metro station on line 3 (L3, green). It is a few minutes' walk from the metro exit. The camp starts at 9:00.

Can a player from outside Barcelona or from another country join?

Yes. The camp takes place in Barcelona, in the mornings only (9:00 to 13:00), and players from outside the city and from other countries can sign up. This website is available in six languages; for questions about your trip, message us on WhatsApp at +34 697 635 183.

What should players bring?

Basketball kit and trainers, a water bottle and the will to work. If the player has allergies or needs medication, please say so in the registration form.

When is the Time Chamber Christmas camp in 2026?

There are two weeks: week 1 is 22, 23 and 24 December (Tuesday to Thursday) and week 2 is 28 to 31 December (Monday to Thursday). Hours are 9:00 to 13:00 every day. You can join one week or both.
